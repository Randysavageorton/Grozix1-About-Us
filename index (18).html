Our story

How Grozix Started

We started Grozix after seeing the same problem many times. Businesses bought search, design, web and ads from separate teams, and those teams rarely worked together. Work was lost between them, and results were hard to measure.

At the same time, buyers began asking ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI for answers. Many businesses were not ready for this change.

We built Grozix to solve both problems. One team plans the work, and one report shows the results.

Our goal has not changed. We help businesses grow in ways they can see and measure.

    Grozix was founded to bring search, design, web and ads together in one team.

  2. [Year]

  4. [Year]
